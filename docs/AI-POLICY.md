# AI design policy

Binding for every AI behavior change. Goal: an AI that is **functional, predictable and hard to exploit**, built from a few general mechanisms instead of many special cases. Read before writing a rule; check it against §7. Thresholds below are the constants in `tools/rules/common.py` (values there win if they differ); hook points and ids are in REVERSING.

## 1. Principles

1. **Evidence first.** Add or change a rule only for a failure seen in a log or proven in code. State the failure and the expected log change up front. No speculative features.
2. **Steer vanilla, don't replace it.** Vanilla has gauges → actions → orders (phases, priorities, fight retreat). Fix its inputs (threat, targets, force size) and fill gaps (hunting, contesting). Rewrite a subsystem only when hooking inputs can't express the fix.
3. **One world model, one power metric.** Every decision reads the shared queries (§3) with power from `HPowerScore.singlePowerScore(HCombatStats.unitCombatStats)`. No private scans, radii or formulas.
4. **One question, one measure.** Every judgment of the same situation (start / keep / commit / concede a plan; the same structure judged by two rules) uses the same function at the same place, so two rules can't contradict each other or a plan start and abort on consecutive checks. Turret silencing is decided only inside `aimod_cover`; callers never emulate it with `exclude`.
5. **Plans are commitments.** A decision holds until a declared trigger fires (§4). No re-deciding on a timer, no flip-flopping.
6. **Concentrate force.** Enough to win with margin, all together; never trickle armies in. Can't gather enough: don't go.
7. **Every army has one owner.** Only a higher ladder step (§5) may take an army. Taking one army from an order before Action cancels the whole order in vanilla (`take-keep` spares our Military orders of ≥ 4 armies down to 3/4), so a siege launch never takes from another gathering Military order of 2+ armies (a Defense may). Rules never issue raw unit commands behind an order's back.
8. **Fail safe.** Injected code runs in traps; if it fails or finds nothing, vanilla continues unchanged.
9. **Observable.** Every decision logs one event with inputs, choice and reason code.
10. **Small complexity budget.** One general mechanism over N special cases; delete rules that don't measurably help.

## 2. Decision layers

| Layer | Scope | Cadence | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic | faction | aware scan, posture held ≥ 60 s | director (§5b) over vanilla gauges, aggressiveness, diplomatic targets |
| Operational | army group ↔ one target | aware scan (10 s), acts only on triggers | vanilla orders + our rules (hunt, contest, raid, rally, aborts) |
| Tactical | units in one fight | per tick | vanilla `unitMicroManagement` |

A layer may constrain the one below, never micromanage it.

## 3. World model (per faction, every aware scan)

- **Hostile groups:** at-war armies clustered by proximity (250): power, position, heading, on own territory, supply, intent when known.
- **Threat at a point** `aimod_threat(fac, p, r)`: at-war armies + raiders whose raid targets us within r, plus movers heading to p that arrive within 20 s (≈ 120 units) and whose path ends within r (a shuffle at their own border never enters the circle). Weights: an army occupying a structure > 60 from p counts 0.25 when p is on our land, 0.5 off it, in full when p is a structure of its own faction (its Defense outranks its capture); for p on our land an idle army off our land > 100 away counts 0.5; full at their own position / capture. Heal / retreat / strand safety uses the undiscounted threat (an army rests there; a busy stack is free once its capture ends).
- **Diplomacy:** combat only at `War`; Truce / Tribute / Alliance forbid it, Hostility never fights. Threat = at-war armies only, read live; a declaration or truce is a trigger for every running plan.
- **Shared queries** (`rules/world.py`; every rule uses them): `aimod_threat`, `aimod_threat_now` (3 s), `aimod_react` (+ free at-war armies within REACT_R 480; capturing ones at half), `aimod_neutral` (raiders busy with someone else: they fight back), `aimod_hthreat` (hunt: the prey's side; its allies only if they arrive before the kill), `aimod_own`, `aimod_terrain`, `aimod_land` (distance to our land), `aimod_supok` / `aimod_short`, `aimod_sieged`, `aimod_cover` (turret cover, §5a; each structure read in its own trap: one that throws counts 0, logged `cvbad`), `aimod_defend`, `aimod_danger` (place memory). `threat` is armies only; rules add `aimod_cover` where structures matter. `aimod_home` (hostile power free to strike our land: director spare force, raid home race, launch gate `exposed`) counts a home guard at OUT_W, like `aimod_threat` for a point on our land: judged where the army will stand (a stander's position, a mover's path end, as the rally's standoff test): on its own faction's zone and > OUT_R from every structure on our land. A Patrol inside its own land is no strike; a mover heading for our border or leaving its land, or with no path end, counts in full. A total below HOME_MIN_H (= RALLY_MIN_H 80k) counts 0: a raider band the village militia hold is no strike (as for the rally). The rally's active test and rally-point safety count a mover only if its path ends inside the radius (arrive variant): border shuffles aren't attacks.
- **Our land** = our structures on zones we own (UWHeadquarters in others' zones don't count).

### 3a. Intel (place memory and fog of war built)

Built: per faction, zone → last danger event (our harvester attacked); `aimod_danger` = 1 fading to 0 over DANGER_T 180 s; harvesting teams avoid dangerous fields. Grows one event kind / consumer at a time, each from a logged failure; every rule reads the same `aimod_danger`.

**Fog of war (built, `rules/intel.py`):** our rules judge hostile armies by what the faction sees (`Entity.isVisibleForFaction`: team vision, cloaked enemies need detection) plus a memory of sightings. Every INTEL_T 1 s per AI faction each visible hostile army (at-war owner or raider) writes its record (time, position, power, busy flag, land distance). Rules:
- Shared queries (`aimod_threat` family, `aimod_neutral`, `aimod_home`): a visible army is judged live; an unseen one by its record within SEEN_T 90 s (SEEN_HOME_T 300 s when it was last seen within SEEN_HOME_R 150 of our land: a stack capturing our conceded village that went into stealth is still there for us; a new sighting elsewhere replaces the record), assumed min(GHOST_SPD 3 u/s × age, GHOST_R_MAX 90) closer to the query point than where it was seen (uncapped, every army seen in 3 min within ~800 read as present: rallies ×4 and no en-route strike in a match), at its remembered power (× BUSY_W while last seen busy < BUSY_SEEN_T 30 s ago where the query discounts busy armies; `aimod_home` skips them). Not underestimating: a stack seen nearby keeps counting after it leaves vision.
- Owned siege targets (vanilla sizing, `getEnemyCombatStats`): the owner's armies by vanilla's zone weights (AI_PowerScore_EnemyAdditionalArmy_Factor) at their known zone; armies never seen or forgotten count at the owner's main base (a player assumes the unseen army is home); busy ones × BUSY_W.
- `aimod_fpow(F, obs)` (press / DMZ): Σ power of F's armies as obs last saw them (any age: the stack still exists); unseen never.
- Structures not ours: turret cover only when surveyed (`isReconnedFaction`); siege state of another faction's / a neutral village only while its cell is visible.
- Targets need sight: hunt group members, strike targets (out of sight = `ran`), heal pursuit, anti-air checks.
- Vanilla's own AI (target scoring, micro, fight balance) still sees everything; out of scope. The testbed runs with fog on (`noFog: false`; the K macro also blinds every AI).

### 3b. Diplomacy goals (part 1 built)

`B(F)` = our strength / perceived(F): ≥ 1.5 in charge, ≤ 0.67 losing. Refuse a peace offer from F while our order on F's structure is in Engage / Action (**built**: `peace-gate`, unless one of our structures is besieged), or while in charge and the offer pays less than what we'd take by force (design). Offer peace when losing or when a stronger third enemy appears (never instead of defending). Declare war only with a goal. Hysteresis as §4. **Force peace (built, `fpeace`):** a faction with Allow_PeaceForce (Atreides) imposes a Non-aggression Pact on the at-war faction besieging a village whose defense we gave up (`dhl` < 30 s or rally concession), never on Fremen (FP_SKIP: they break it for free), never without a siege on us, ≤ 1 try per FP_RETRY 60 s. **Treaty scope (built):** a treaty between two other factions cancels none of our orders; one we sign cancels only our orders on the other party (vanilla cancels every order on either party, ours included). Vanilla accepts by trade value only and offers peace at status 0 by random chance.

## 4. Plans, ratios, triggers

A plan = a vanilla `AIOrder` plus our enter / keep / abort thresholds and triggers.

**Ratios** (ours / theirs at the place; × terrain first):

| Name | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| keep | 1.0 | continue; also "at least even" (DEF_HOPE_IN, rally commit, opening EARLY_REQ, tension floor) |
| neutral | 1.25 | siege launch on a neutral target (NEUTRAL_REQ; vanilla 1.0); supply hysteresis SUP_ENTER |
| enter | 1.5 | start an offensive; rally ends at enter × 1.15 |
| abort | 0.7 | re-decide below this (also the press keep) |
| retreat | 0.65 | fight retreat (fixed; vanilla re-rolls a range every tick); the no-enemy 1.0 is never multiplied |
| PART_X | 2 × enter | engage with part of the order still on the way |
| HUNT_TO | 2.5 | hunt force target |
| KILL | 3 | force target of raids / sieges (RAID_TO, JOIN_TO), director softness (PRESS), restricted hunts (far, under cover, deep desert, defensive posture) |
| terrain | × 1.3 own zone, × 0.8 at-war zone; off our zone also × (1 − life decay/day × 2.5 days) in regions that drain ground life (Acid Lakes 0.75, The Desolation 0.4 floor); 1 at a sietch / renegade base (its garrison neither heals nor drains) | attack on their land needs ≈ 1.9; home defense holds to ≈ 0.5 |

Entry and exit measure the same thing at the same place (hunt: the prey at start, then the group's core = the member nearest our land; never a centroid). Members > LOCAL from the core leave the target group.

**Timers** (commitments and cooldowns; a plan's own triggers can end it earlier):

| Tier | Uses |
|---|---|
| 10-15 s | CRUSH_T 10, PURSUIT_T 15, HEAL_COMMIT 15 |
| 20-30 s | gather hold 20 per attack, worm hold 20, raid gauge hold RAID_GAUGE_T 20, HUNT_GAP 30, commit 30, DISC_RELAUNCH 30, objective switch 30 |
| 60-90 s | HUNT_AREA_T 60, DISC_RETRY 60, RAID_RETRY 60, RALLY_GIVEUP 60, posture 60, RIDE_MAX 60, GIVEUP_T 90, RALLY_COOL 90 |
| 120 s + backoff | PRESS_RETRY 120, PRESS_MAX 240, retry 30 → 240, stuck 120 → 480 |

**Supply budget:** an army needs 0.35 × distance to our land + SUP_RESERVE (≈ 17, 10 s of drain) to walk home (drain ≈ 0.28 / unit). **Supply decisions use absolute supply against the walk / fight they face, never a share of max supply** (max supply ranges 65-440 by faction and upgrades; a max-supply rise drops the share with nothing spent). Exceptions, by design: the occupation refill (data: a share of max) and `release` / siege picks, which mirror vanilla's 90% Resupply trigger and keep margin for the militia fight's drain. Hunt and contest armies need ≥ 90% life and the trip budget; vanilla Defense picks of our structures take any army not `short`; vanilla's Resupply skips an army that refills where it stands (`Army.isGainingSupply`, life above the trigger). `short` = losing supply and below it: hunts abort (in contact too, unless on the prey with KILL ×, never for a powerless prey); in a fight the balance × (1 − 0.6 × short share). Joining a hunt needs SUP_ENTER × the budget at the target. **Recall:** every army of ours in a fight short and none on a Military order → balance 0 (leave, even winning). **Stranded:** a short army that can't pay the walk home anyway gets neither penalty nor recall: withdrawal is impossible, so it fights all-in (below). Our supply budget assumes the normal drain rate (deep desert is faster: open issue).

**Re-decide only on triggers:** (1) a new hostile group can reach our armies or target before we finish; (2) the projected ratio crosses abort; (3) the target is invalid (dead, owner changed, invisible, a hunt prey home); (4) stuck: no progress for N s (chase: PURSUIT_T without the prey losing PROGRESS 10% power or us closing max(CLOSE 30, (gap − CATCH_R 40) × PURSUIT_T / CATCH_T 30); within CATCH_R counts as progress); (5) expiry.

**On a trigger, in order:** continue if projected ≥ keep (threat ETA included) → reinforce if free armies arrive in time → withdraw to the nearest safe point if we get there before contact → fight all-in if withdrawal is impossible, or ratio ≥ abort and the objective is worth it (own structure, nearly done capture) → otherwise withdraw.

## 5. Priority ladder

| Prio | Orders |
|---|---|
| 10 | defend main base (vanilla) |
| 5 | Defense of own structure (vanilla), hunts: contest of a siege near us, chase, neutral-raider hunt (only when no at-war hunt qualifies) |
| 4 | Raze |
| 3 | Annex |
| 1 | Pillage (vanilla and `raid`), Liberate |
| 0 | Patrol, Resupply, Discovery, `strand` |

New rules pick a priority here and add themselves. An army in Resupply is never free. A short army on hostile land starts nothing.

## 5a. Faction goals (highest first)

A lower goal starts only when every higher one is satisfied or impossible; a running lower plan yields when a higher one appears (except a fight about to be won in contact).

| # | Goal | Means | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Survive / supply | supply budget, `strand` (idle army on hostile land → Patrol home; next to our structure but still losing supply in the neighbouring zone → moved into its safe position), `undeploy` (installed F_Special_2 off-fight on hostile land), `ability-gate` (no emergency abilities at balance 1.0) | short army starts nothing |
| 2 | Defend what we own | `aimod_defend` (below): contest hunt, free armies within GATHER_R 900 join; our pillages and Annexes within 500 yield when the defenders fall short (walking: cancelled; occupation under way: split, one army finishes); `rally` when outmatched | defensive: no new vanilla siege, no chase starts, chases abort (`defend`); exception: prey in contact with ≥ KILL ×; short of the ratio, gather at home, never trickle in |
| 3 | Bunker | a lost village under our main base (≤ 110): vanilla Annex redirected to it; a neutral one × 1.5 in the Annex value; a village pairing with ours (≤ 130): target score × 1.3 | – |
| 4 | Stop captures next to us | contest hunt on an enemy siege ≤ 500 from our land (weight 3); a running chase yields (`objective`) | – |
| 5 | Opportunity | chase hunt ≤ 500 from our land (≤ 300 if the prey is on its own land) | – |
| 5a | Neutral raiders | neutral hunt near our land; elsewhere only in contact at KILL ×; never raiders whose raid targets a faction at war with us (they are hostile only to their target and do our work: attack the enemy, don't help it) | – |
| 5b | Raid | `raid` (below) | – |
| 6 | Expand | vanilla Annex / Pillage / Raze, spaced and sized (below) | – |

**Vanilla Defense** (`checkStructures`) requires a fixed 1.15 (data: VillageDefense Min = Max, Insane and Medium; vanilla re-rolled 1.0-3.5 every 0.5 s) = enter on our own land (vanilla's estimate has no terrain factor).

**Sieges near us (`aimod_sieged`):** an at-war faction's siege / occupation of a neutral village, ours, or a non-at-war faction's; a raider siege only of a neutral village or ours.

**Defend (`aimod_defend`):** our village or main base besieged by an at-war faction or taken over by renegades, else a neutral village ≤ HOME_RING_R 250 of our active main base besieged / occupied by one. Several at once: the highest stake wins (a capture: Annex / Liberate / Takeover, or a siege whose occupation hasn't started, over a pillage (`getOccupationActionKind` "Pillage"); then the nearest to our active main base). Skipped when hopeless (never while an order of ours defending it / aimed within GATHER_R of it has an army in transit, e.g. a worm ride: they read 0 power; all ours within GATHER_R + cover, × terrain, < DEF_HOPE_IN × threat + neutral raiders + enemy cover, DEF_HOPE_COMMIT 0.7 within RALLY_COOL of a rally commit; re-included at DEF_HOPE_OUT 1.2; map `dhl`) or conceded by the rally; never skips a main base. A running Defense of a hopeless or conceded structure is stopped (`dstop`) and gets no armies (vanilla re-sends fled armies one by one). Raider sieges are the contest hunt's, not a posture.

**Turret awareness.** Main base guns (power 80, range 80) cover COVER_R 130 from the base centre. A turret (any built, powered building with an attack, in a village or a main base: MissileBattery 40, range 80) covers a point from where it stands: in full within its own attack range, fading to 0 TURRET_SPREAD 40 beyond (a battery at the edge of a fight counts a fraction; a powered building without an attack never). A village under a faction's siege has silent turrets (both views). Attack sizing adds every other at-war structure covering the target and drops the target's own while besieged; defense sizing likewise. Main-base guns count MB_GUN_W 3× everywhere (their power score understates them). Enemy cover always counts in full. Our cover counts in full when we defend (rally, contest, hunts, home need, vanilla defense sizing), × OWN_COVER_ATK 0.5 on an attack target (siege-join / siege-engage, raid launch / keep, director press start / keep: a battery hits only within ~80 of where it stands and doesn't follow the fight) and not at all at a sietch / renegade base. Hunts count enemy cover as threat and ours as power in the evaluation; a chase under enemy cover only in contact with KILL × (else `turret`). *Unverified:* whether a raider siege (no `besiegingFaction`) silences turrets; `aimod_cover` counts them.

### Force size

- **Hunts:** nearest free armies until HUNT_TO × their side (prey + `aimod_react` + enemy cover) / terrain (KILL restricted); past HUNT_CAP 2 × prey group + 1 armies stop once enter holds. Armies alone fill goal and gate (cover counts in the evaluation only: the prey doesn't stay under our guns). Contests take armies until HUNT_TO however many (all in reach if short). The force must still have enter × their side (KILL restricted), else no hunt; a contest goes short of that but not below DEF_HOPE_IN × their side (the defense's hopeless measure: rally / Defense own it); the candidate test and the `objective` abort apply the same floor.
- **Sieges (`siege-join`):** vanilla's candidates without armies whose walk to the target fails vanilla's siege supply check (SUP_U × distance > 0.7 × supply: one such army cancels the whole order at once; not while a free Supply Drop is held), vanilla's pick, then the nearest idle armies within JOIN_R 450 that pass the supply budget (home from the target, and the walk there) join until JOIN_TO × (threat + enemy cover + militia + other raiders) / terrain. Required NEUTRAL_REQ on neutral targets (SITE_REQ 2.5 on a sietch or a renegade base: the garrison (< 8 / < 10 units) doesn't respawn, but the sietch's random harass tick (3 units, unrelated to the fight) can fire during a strike; ENTER lost every logged strike, at launch and at the target; their pick also needs at least SIETCH_ARMIES 8 / RENEGADE_ARMIES 10 armies, the garrison they always spawn, else it is emptied: `sfloor`; vanilla's simulated re-pick then waits (NotEnoughArmies) while we own that many fighting armies, else it is emptied too and vanilla recruits (ArmyNotStrongEnough)); after joining, a sietch / renegade-base pick still below SITE_REQ × their side by our measure (armies only: no own turret cover, terrain 1; same at the target) is emptied (`sweak`; a pass logs `sgo`): vanilla's own estimate passes picks we put at 1.4, EARLY_REQ while we own < 2 villages (the opening's 2 armies take the first villages). Vanilla sizing counts every at-war army near the target, not only the owner's.
- **Owned targets:** a siege launch on an at-war village needs requiredPowerBalance ≥ OWNED_REQ 1.3 × (1 + (its distance from our land − OWNED_D0 200) / OWNED_DK 600) (a deep attack can't retreat: Zanit 415 away lost 7 of 12, Sinwaz 877 away 4 of 8) (siege-join raises vanilla's; 35 such attacks that reached the fight: req < 1.25 won 0 of 7 and lost 18 armies, est < 1.3 won 0 of 5).
- **Square law vs militia (`rules/sqlaw.py`):** `aimod_sqr(armies, village)` = (Σ offensivePotential × Σ health) of our armies / the same over its militia squads. Siege-join adds armies until it reaches SQ_MIN 1.5 (as well as JOIN_TO × their side); a raid picks armies until it does, else skips that village for RAID_RETRY (log `raid refuse why sq`). 743 militia fights: ≥ 1.5 won 97%, below 39-61% even with the summed ratio ≥ 1.75 (2 armies vs 3-6 squads).
- **Raids:** nearest raid-ready armies until RAID_TO × their side; the first one must walk (flyers can't occupy). Hunts (not contests) on prey standing on its own faction's land use the same floor at that zone's village. On an owned (at-war) village armies within REACT_R get no RAID_FAR discount (launch ENTER × all of their side, abort ABORT × all): the owner defends it rather than "may come" (Smugglers' Liberate of Nunesek started at 1.15× and lost 3 armies in 15 s). On an at-war village the armies part of their side (launch and abort) is at least `aimod_owner_pw`: the owner's combat armies as we know them (seen → its zone, remembered → where seen, unknown → its main base; vanilla's owner factors by zones, busy × BUSY_W). Atreides raided Smugglers' Tab-Al'nin twice on 274k vs a seen 287k and lost every army to 166-245M of order power.
- **Ground army (`airpick`):** a siege pick (real or simulated) without a ground army gets the nearest ground army of vanilla's candidates added, else it is emptied (vanilla waits / recruits): flyers (`Unit.isFlying`: ships, frigates) never occupy, a ship-only siege stood at Tab-Al'lon for 8+ min.

### Gathering and engage

A moving prey (army) is never waited for and never idled next to (it walks past or runs); a structure can't run: gather strength next to it, out of its reach (`stage`: every siege army's regroup point ≥ STAGE_R 130 from the target on its approach side, set once it is within STAGE_SEE 320: turret reach, the militia trigger is almost at the village, and no loitering in it; fast armies wait there too), then go in together. Never wait in the deep desert. A hunt leaves Regroup once the armies within LOCAL of the core pass enter (a contest: its whole force, sized for it at start; the capture runs meanwhile); stragglers walk in. Our sieges (`siege-engage`) leave Regroup once the armies within ENGAGE_R 160 of the target (staged or there) pass enter (neutral 1.25) vs threat + enemy cover + militia; PART_X × that while some are still on the way. In Engage a leader > 15 ahead of the farthest attack army (within 400) waits out of contact, ≤ 20 s per attack (`gather`); a contest's leaders wait only while the farthest army's walk + 20 s still ends before the capture (`_cap_rem`). **Ring:** while the contested capture advances with none of ours inside village radius + 20 (the only place that stops its progress), every contest army within LOCAL of the village and not fighting walks to radius × RING_K from its centre (log hunt `ring`; re-sent at most every RING_MOVE_T 5 s per army, never to one already walking into the ring). Siege picks don't take armies on a Discovery trip farther than JOIN_R from the target. Siege judgments count neutral raiders at the village with its militia.

### Rally

When one of our structures D is actively threatened (an at-war army at it or heading there; a stack standing or shuffling inside its own land is a standoff, not an attack; neutral raiders at D, e.g. rebels of a rebelling village, are the contest hunt's / vanilla Defense's, never a rally) by ≥ RALLY_MIN_H 80k that our defenders within RALLY_R 600 + our turrets, × terrain, can't beat by enter: every defender not in contact walks to one rally point R (main base if clear within 150, else our nearest structure ≥ 150 from D and clear; a point whose walk from the defenders' centroid passes within CROSS_R 150 of D is on the enemy's side and loses to any on theirs), its Resupply / Defense / Patrol dropped (found in the order list: Army.aiOrder is unreliable; a Defense of D's surroundings is dropped even at R) and vanilla kept from re-sending it (a Defense of D's surroundings gets only armies already within RALLY_HERE of D and not at R until the rally ends); structures within 150 of D aren't heal / flee targets. **Walk through the enemy:** a heal / flee / `strand` target whose straight walk passes within CROSS_R 150 of our structure under at-war siege (threat ≥ RALLY_MIN_H within LOCAL) or of D is rated overwhelming (aimod_unsafe), unless the army already stands within 150 of it (that fight is the retreat's): between an enemy capture and the far village, our side (main base, villages behind us) is safer. Defenses of other structures (> 150 from D) keep their armies. Our armies on a Military mission count as defenders only within RALLY_HERE of D and are never moved. Ends at enter × 1.15: the contest hunt or vanilla's Defense takes them in together. A D that misses one pass (the active test flickers as a stack shuffles at ~FLEE_R) keeps the rally RALLY_OFF_T 10 s while our side there is still short; a rally back on within RALLY_RESUME 30 s of its last qualifying pass keeps its RALLY_GIVEUP clock (no endless rally / off cycle). The threat at D is `aimod_react` + at-war turret cover at D (as the hopeless test counts it), so `here` / commit and `dhope` judge the same sides.
- **Here:** defenders already within RALLY_HERE 250 of D, not counting those at the running rally point (the gathered force) (+ our turrets there, × terrain), at least even → commit at once.
- **Timeout:** after RALLY_GIVEUP 60 s still short: commit if the gathered force is at least even (always for a main base), else concede D for RALLY_COOL 90 s (and its neighbours within LOCAL). A D judged hopeless < 30 s ago is never committed (here) and is conceded at the timeout.
- **After a commit:** D and its neighbours within LOCAL get no rally for RALLY_COOL (a neighbour's rally would walk the committed force away); vanilla's Defense of D takes the whole army within RALLY_R of D (`allIn`: every considered army, not just enough for its own estimate; vanilla's idle list has no distance limit, so it is cut to the rally's radius first; log `dall`), and D is conceded only below DEF_HOPE_COMMIT 0.7 for RALLY_COOL (a commit at even must not flip to hopeless at even seconds later).

### Hunts

A contest whose enemy group core (the member nearest our land, where the abort pass judges it) stands under enemy turret cover isn't started (`nogo turret`: it aborted `turret` on the next check). A contest that can't arrive before the capture ends (nearest free army / 6 per s > remaining time from the observed progress rate + 10 s) isn't started and doesn't trigger `objective`. Score = threat / (nearest free army distance + 100)²: the enemy in front of us first (half the distance needs ~¼ the threat); every contest outranks every chase (goal 4 over 5, no `objective` flip), in stake bands (× CONTEST_W): a capture 1, a pillage CONTEST_PIL_W 0.5, + CONTEST_HOME_W 0.25 within DEF_NEAR_R 300 of our active main base (home captures > far captures > home pillages > far pillages); while `aimod_defend` holds a capture of ours, a pillage elsewhere starts no contest (its armies are the capture's), except a **minor** one: a village not ours (neutral / a third faction's) farther than CONTEST_NEAR 300 from our land is scored like a chase and never triggers `objective`. An objective-anchored plan ends when the fight drifts from our land (`drift`); a contest ends (`won`) once its village is no longer besieged and the group core is WON_R 150 from it (the beaten group isn't followed home; its members are barred for GIVEUP_T); a chase aborts (`objective`) when its armies could contest a siege near our land, then only contests start for 30 s. A hunt starts only when the trip (walk out + way back) fits the supply budget. After a `supply` / `weak` / `turret` / `desert` abort, no chase within LOCAL for HUNT_AREA_T. A prey running on its own land is followed only while a member is below CHASE_LIFE 0.35 or we are on it (CATCH_R) at enter (a village annexed mid-fight turns the zone theirs). An at-war main base within BASE_KEEP 250 of the prey adds its defense × (1 − d/250) to the threat (start and abort alike); the "we crush it" supply waiver lasts CRUSH_T of prey running, summed. `chase` abort (trigger 4) bars the runners for GIVEUP_T. Deep desert prey: start only in contact at KILL ×, abort `desert` once the core is there. The first Engage target is the armed enemy nearest the anchor (a powerless member only if none is armed). Group = the prey faction's armies within LOCAL of the anchor; with no prey faction (raider-siege contest, neutral hunt) raiders only. An order in transit (worm, shuttle) isn't re-judged until it lands (≤ RIDE_MAX).

## 5b. Strategic director (`rules/strat.py`)

Goals 1-2 stay reflexes; the director decides what the **spare** armies do from goal 3 down. Why: vanilla targets an enemy village only at aggressiveness ≥ 50 and desiredStatus ≥ 1 toward its owner, so a faction at war with a weak neighbour never touches its villages and the mid game idles.

**Built** (every scan, posture held ≥ 60 s unless a higher one triggers, `strat` rows): spare force S = free armies (not defending, not resupplying below RAID_LIFE) minus the home need ENTER × `aimod_home` / OWN_T, less our turret cover: at each structure of ours with at-war power standing within STAND_R, min(cover, ENTER × that power) / OWN_T (`cv`), one credit per turret cluster (structures within COVER_R share batteries). Postures, first match wins:
1. **defend**: our structure besieged.
2. **recover**: worn power (life < RAID_LIFE or short) > 0.5 × total, left below 0.35: no new press; `raid` only raids that are a recovery (village nearer than home for every raid army, RAID_TO). Hunts, vanilla sieges and defense aren't gated.
3. **hold**: no spare force.
4. **press E**: the nearest soft village of an at-war E within FRONT_R 600 (spare armies within 450 have PRESS × hold(v) = `aimod_react` + enemy cover + militia, / terrain; softness falls to enter with contact tension), B(E) ≥ 1. Annex if in supply range, available and affordable (gates lifted, score × PRESS_W, E's other villages dropped), else pillage via `raid`. Keep = our power within PRESS_R + cover ≥ PRESS_KEEP × their side (while our occupation of it progresses, their armies only within max(LOCAL, (remaining s + CONTEST_SLACK) × CONTEST_SPD), any heading, none from outside: what arrives before it ends; a stalled one uses the full test; `slow` never drops a progressing occupation); dropped on done / gone / Devastated / truce / PRESS_MAX (`slow`) / weak, then skipped PRESS_RETRY, our orders on it cancelled.
5. **expand** / **harass**: vanilla Annex; `raid` and hunts.

**Planned** (each verified before the next): utility ranking U = value × swing / time (swing 2 for enemy villages; pillage = loot + their halved production − our doubled Annex cost) across neutral Annex, enemy Annex and pillage, with an expiry of 1.5 × estimated time; home race on every offensive; front patrols (never idle > 60 s with spare armies). Anti-exploit targets: kiting (`chase` / `pursuit`, built), turret baits (cover, built), pull-away (home race), split raids (defend the most value at risk first), predictability (keep vanilla's random top pick), omniscience (§3a).

## 5c. Contact tension (`rules/tension.py`)

Contact points are worth more than their production: whoever holds both has a bunker pair and a safe border. Pair = our village V and the nearest other-owned or neutral village W within TEN_R 140 (main bases excluded). Tension T(V) ∈ [0, 1], updated every TEN_T: + TEN_UP × closeness (1 at ≤ 60 → 0.25 at 140), × TEN_HOT 2 while W's owner's armies stand within COVER_R of V or anyone besieges W; − TEN_DOWN once the pair is gone. T only scales existing thresholds; posture order and supply rules still win; never against a stronger owner (B ≥ 1).
1. **Contest (built):** a siege of W next to V → contest hunt at entry enter → keep as T goes 0 → 1, even while our rally gathers elsewhere.
2. **Take (built):** Annex value × (1 + T) for a neutral W; press softness PRESS → enter for an enemy W. **Gate:** at war and T ≥ TEN_GATE, W's owner is listed for vanilla Annex / Pillage at desiredStatus 0, only W kept; a launch on W creating no order while an army was free halves T (TEN_FAIL).
3. **Truce (design, after 1-2 verified):** no attack on a truce partner by default (a default, not an invariant). Fremen (no Standing: treason is nearly free) may press at T 1 held T_HOLD. Others: T 1 for T_HOLD, `checkDeclareWar` Success, B ≥ 1, W soft → let the target through; vanilla declares war itself on the launch (REVERSING "War declaration"), so no second declaration path. One per faction per long cooldown; logged.

## 5d. DMZ (`rules/dmz.py`)

Territorial: border region of E = a region E owns next to one of ours (village-less ones too); border village = E's village (not a main base) in one. Counted per faction pair every DMZ_T 5 s; no distance terms.
- **War, E holds ≥ DMZ_WAR 2 border regions (DMZ on):** its border villages are taken, never burnt (a pillage devastates them for 20 days and doubles our Annex cost there). E is listed for vanilla's siege scans despite desiredStatus 0 (getTargetStatus lift); the target scores keep only its border (and contact-partner) villages, drop them from Pillage, Annex / Liberate scores × DMZ_W 1.5; `raid` liberates them (Liberate available and affordable; Just Freed: neutral, untargetable 20 days, then ours to annex; unaffordable: waits), else leaves them unless pressed for pillage (Liberate not available at all); the press ranks them first (distance × DMZ_PREF 0.5) and annexes when it can, else the raid liberates.
- **Peace / truce:** two border villages are tolerable. E holding ≥ DMZ_PEACE 3, or 2 while besieging a neutral village in a region next to ours (about to take a third), breaks the truce: vanilla `declareWar` (Influence; refused in the peace-offer grace period, with our units in E's land, an ally, a liege), then the contest hunt stops the capture. Not while we defend, not vs a stronger E (our army power ≥ DMZ_B 1.0 × E's; Fremen DMZ_B_FREMEN 0.8: no Standing to lose), one try per pair per DMZ_COOL 120 s.

## 6. Code rules

- One concern per module (README "Code structure"); a rule = one function: read the model, decide, issue at most one order, log one event.
- Thresholds are named constants in `rules/common.py` (§4 values where one applies).
- Rules run in the aware scan or a CHECK tick; nothing per game tick; worst case O(armies²) per scan.
- Reuse vanilla entry points (`addOrder`, `tryArmyAction`, `pickUnits`) so its bookkeeping stays consistent.
- Offline check: both boot files patch and re-parse (no bulk decompiles). In game: `HEALTH OK`, then the target metric.

## 7. Checklist for a new rule

1. Which logged failure does it fix; which `mod log` number should change?
2. Which layer and priority?
3. Shared world model only (no private scan)?
4. Enter / keep / abort and triggers; can it flip-flop?
5. Does another rule judge the same situation? Then use the same measure (§1.4), or one of them will contradict the other.
6. Can it loop with another rule or vanilla (hunt → lose → retreat → hunt)? What breaks the loop?
7. Target moves, disappears or runs home?
8. Concentrates force (no trickle)?
9. Log event and reason code?
10. On error or nothing found, does vanilla continue?
